PRIVACY POLICY

Your game stays yours.

Effective October 5, 2026 · Operator: Jintao Lv

Information collected by the app

Number Grove does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. The app has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases, social features, location access, or third-party tracking. Its gameplay works offline.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game boards, scores, earned gems, theme progress, daily challenge results, tutorial completion and preferences in its private local storage. These records are used only to provide the game and restore progress. The app does not operate a cloud synchronization service. Apple device backups may include app data according to the device settings you choose.

Your choices

You can turn sounds and haptics off, reduce motion, change language and reset game progress in Settings. Resetting progress deletes local game records and retains your preferences. Removing the app may remove its local data. Number Grove has no server-side account to delete.

Support and this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its host may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy policy. Opening a link from the app uses your browser. The website contains no advertising or analytics scripts set by the developer.

If you submit a support issue, we use the information you voluntarily provide to address the issue. GitHub issues are public and governed by GitHub's privacy statement. Do not submit sensitive personal information. For privacy or support requests, use the contact route on our support page.

Children and changes

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players. If future features change these practices, this policy will be updated with a new effective date before those changes apply.
