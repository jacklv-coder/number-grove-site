A few helpful connections.

Support for Number Grove: 2248 Puzzle, a free offline game for iPhone and iPad.

How do I connect tiles?

Hold one tile and drag to a matching neighbor. After the first two matching tiles, continue with the same number or double the last tile. Horizontal, vertical and diagonal neighbors all work. Slide back to the previous tile to remove your last selection. Lift your finger to merge.

How are numbers and scores calculated?

The selected numbers are added and rounded up to a power of two. For example, 2 + 2 + 2 becomes 8. Points reward chain length and growth relative to the board's current number range. The resulting tile number and the points earned are different. Points record session performance and personal bests; they cannot buy tools. This version does not include an online leaderboard.

How do levels work?

Your biggest number across Classic and Daily sets your player level: 512 is Level 2, 1024 is Level 3, 2048 is Level 4, and 4096 is Level 5. Points and gems do not determine level. Level 2 unlocks Daybreak; gem rewards begin at Level 3. Undo and a fresh board preserve your level. Reset all progress returns to Level 1. Tap the level strip to see the next target and rewards.

What are gems?

First reach 1024 to earn 30 gems, 2048 for 35, and 4096 for 40. Each next power of two adds 5 to the reward, capped at 60 gems per milestone. Each milestone pays once across Classic and Daily; a jump includes intermediate milestones. Ordinary merges award points only. Undo keeps earned gems and records without paying the same reward again. Undo costs 80 gems, Prune 100, and Shuffle 100. Hints and no-move rescue reshuffles are free. New players start with 80 gems. Gems cannot be purchased in this release; older leaf balances convert one-for-one.

Where are the other game screens?

Use the grid button in the top-left corner for Daily Seed, your garden themes, records and help. Tap the gem balance or goal strip to see milestone rewards.

How does Daily Seed work?

Each UTC date has a deterministic starting board and 30 moves. Gem-spending tools are unavailable in this mode. Completing the challenge awards 20 gems once per date and records your score. A new challenge begins at 00:00 UTC. A new day's challenge replaces the previous unfinished daily board when you open Daily Seed.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved automatically in this app's private storage on your device. Number Grove does not provide an account or its own cloud sync. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. Removing the app or resetting progress can remove local data.

Adjust sound, motion and language

Open Settings to change sounds, touch feedback, reduced motion or language. Your Garden shows available themes and the number milestones that unlock them. All themes are free to earn.
